Turnbuckle Alley · art direction

Three looks for one small wrestling town

Each direction paints the same two moments: Main Street at golden hour, and Saturday night inside the Sportatorium. Everything is drawn in code at 384×216 and scaled up pixel-perfect. Open one, then press 1 and 2 (or tap the buttons) to switch scenes.

A

Golden Hour Storybook

A warm, painterly picture book. Shadows lean plum and teal instead of black, outlines take on the colour of what they wrap, and skies melt in soft dithered bands. It rewards slow looking: flower boxes, chalkboard specials, framed photos in the diner window.

Open Direction A →Saturday night
B

Saturday Morning Cartoon

An original 90s wrestling cartoon that never aired. Thick dark outlines, flat cel shading, big heads about two and a half heads tall, leaning buildings and enormous boots. Loud, bouncy and readable from across the room, or on a phone held at arm's length.

Open Direction B →Saturday night
C

Halftone Wrestling Magazine

Print nostalgia brought to life: thirteen colours only, off-white paper, ink outlines and halftone dots doing all the shading. Spot colours of hot pink, cyan and gold pop like an old wrestling magazine, fight poster or photocopied fan zine.
